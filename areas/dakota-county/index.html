Residential Appraisals in Dakota County, Minnesota

First Class Appraisal started in Burnsville. Before moving to Prior Lake, I lived in Burnsville for more than 20 years, and my kids went to school in the Burnsville and Lakeville school districts. I know these neighborhoods the way only a longtime resident can.

I am also the broker of WHY USA Suburban Realty, which started in Burnsville and expanded to Lakeville and Bloomington between 2002 and 2008. Between appraising and brokerage, I have watched this market from both sides for a long time.

Today I appraise homes across all of Dakota County, from Burnsville, Lakeville, Apple Valley and Eagan to Farmington and the older river town housing in Hastings.

What I look for in Dakota County

The county covers a wide range of housing ages and styles. A few things that often come up here:

  • 1970s and 1980s split levels and two stories in Burnsville, Apple Valley and Eagan, where updating and condition drive a lot of the value difference.
  • New and recent construction in Lakeville and Farmington, where builder pricing and lot premiums need to be checked against resales.
  • Older homes in Hastings, where age, character and updates have to be weighed carefully.
  • Lake property on Crystal Lake, Orchard Lake and Lake Marion, which I know well from my years living in the area, plus pond lots that can carry a premium when the market supports it.

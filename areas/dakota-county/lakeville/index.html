Lakeville Home Appraisals

Lakeville is one of the busiest housing markets in the south metro, and it is a market I know personally. I lived next door in Burnsville for more than 20 years, my kids went to school in the Lakeville and Burnsville school districts, and WHY USA Suburban Realty, where I am the broker, expanded into Lakeville in the 2000s.

I appraise homes across the city for buyers, homeowners, attorneys and lenders.

New construction and fast growth

A large share of Lakeville's homes are newer, and new neighborhoods keep coming. That makes careful appraisal work important:

  • Builder prices and incentives need to be checked against resales of similar homes
  • Lot premiums for walkout, pond, wooded or larger lots need market support
  • Upgrades and finished basements need to be valued at what buyers actually pay for them

Lake homes too

Lakeville also has lakeshore and lake access homes on Lake Marion, Orchard Lake and Crystal Lake, lakes I got to know well during my years in the area. I value frontage, water quality and access with the care these properties need.

Appraisals I do in Lakeville

  • Lakefront and waterfront appraisals
  • Estate and probate appraisals
  • Divorce appraisals
  • Property tax appeal appraisals

Residential Appraisals in Scott County, Minnesota

Scott County is home base for me. I live and work in Prior Lake, so the lake neighborhoods, the newer subdivisions along the 169 and 13 corridors, and the rural acreage toward Jordan and Belle Plaine are places I know firsthand.

I am a Minnesota Certified Residential Appraiser and have been appraising homes since 1991. If you need a value you can rely on in Scott County, I can help.

What I look for in Scott County

Scott County is not one market. A lakeshore home on Prior Lake, a new build in Shakopee and a hobby farm outside Jordan are valued on very different evidence. A few things I pay close attention to here:

  • Lakeshore on Prior Lake, Spring Lake and the smaller lakes, where frontage, water depth, slope and the bay a home sits on can move value a lot.
  • Newer construction, where builder incentives and lot premiums have to be sorted out from true market value.
  • Rural and acreage property in the southern part of the county, where good comparable sales can be far apart and need careful adjustment.
  • Townhome and association communities, where fees and what they cover matter.

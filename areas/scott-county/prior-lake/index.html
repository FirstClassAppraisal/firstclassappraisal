Prior Lake Home Appraisals

Prior Lake is where I live and where my business is based. When you hire me for a Prior Lake appraisal, you get an appraiser who knows the lakes, the neighborhoods and the way homes here actually sell.

Lake homes are their own market

Prior Lake and Spring Lake shoreline is some of the most valuable property in Scott County, and it is also some of the hardest to appraise well. Two homes a few doors apart can be worth very different amounts. I look closely at:

  • Feet of frontage and how usable it is
  • Water depth, shoreline type and slope to the water
  • Which part of the lake the home sits on, and the view
  • Docks, lifts, boathouses and permits
  • Off lake homes with lake access or association rights

These factors need support from real sales, not guesses. That is where 35 years of experience and 14 years as a national review appraiser help.

Off the water too

Most Prior Lake homes are not on the lake, and I appraise those every day as well: established neighborhoods, newer subdivisions, townhomes and acreage on the edges of town.

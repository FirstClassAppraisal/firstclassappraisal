Meet Craig Thornton, Certified Residential Appraiser

I have been valuing Minnesota homes since 1991. For nearly 35 years I have appraised properties of every type, held a real estate broker license for over 25 years, and spent 14 years as a national review appraiser for one of the four largest banks in the country, checking other appraisers' work for accuracy and support.

That mix of field, brokerage and review experience shapes how I work. I know how buyers and sellers think, how lenders read a report, and what makes a valuation stand up when someone challenges it.

Credentials

  • Minnesota Certified Residential Appraiser, license 4003511
  • Licensed real estate broker
  • 35 years of appraisal experience, since 1991
  • 14 years as a national review appraiser

What you can expect: a direct conversation with the appraiser who does the work, a careful inspection, a report that explains its reasoning, and delivery when I said it would arrive.

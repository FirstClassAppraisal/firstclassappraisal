Residential Appraisal Services

Whatever the reason for your valuation, you get a thorough, well-supported report from an appraiser who has done this work since 1991.

Purchase and refinance appraisals

A supported opinion of value for buyers, sellers and lenders. Each report uses current local sales, an on-site inspection and clear reasoning for every adjustment.

Estate and probate valuations

A date-of-death or current value for executors, trustees, attorneys and families. Reports are prepared with the documentation attorneys and executors need.

Divorce and equity-split valuations

An impartial value both sides can understand, prepared for attorneys, mediators and couples who need a fair starting point.

Property tax appeal support

If you believe your assessment is too high, an independent appraisal gives you evidence to bring to the county.

Appraisal review

An experienced second look at an existing appraisal for lenders, attorneys and homeowners. Fourteen years of national review work means I know exactly where reports succeed and where they fall short.

Consulting and pre-listing valuations

A realistic value before you list, refinance or make a major decision, so you can plan with confidence.

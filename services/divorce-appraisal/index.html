Divorce and Equity Split Appraisals

In a divorce, the home is often the largest asset, and both sides need a value they can trust. I provide independent appraisals that are fair, clearly explained and well supported.

Independent and unbiased

My job is to report what the market supports, not to help one side. That is why attorneys, mediators and couples come to me when they need a value that will hold up.

What I can help with

  • Current market value for a buyout or sale
  • Value as of a past date, such as the date of separation
  • Appraisals for one party, both parties or the attorneys
  • Review of an appraisal done by someone else
  • Expert testimony if the case requires it

Common questions

Can one spouse order the appraisal?

Yes. Either spouse, both together or an attorney can engage me. Access to the home is needed for a full interior inspection.

What if we each have a different appraisal?

I can review the other report and explain where and why the values differ.

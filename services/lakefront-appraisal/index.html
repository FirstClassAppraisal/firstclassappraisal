Lakefront and Waterfront Appraisals

Lake homes are some of the hardest properties to appraise well. There are fewer sales, and the features that drive value change from one stretch of shoreline to the next. I live in Prior Lake and have appraised lake property for decades.

What drives lake home value

  • Feet of frontage and how usable the shoreline is
  • Water depth, bottom type and water quality
  • Slope to the water and the view
  • Location on the lake and the size of the lake
  • Docks, lifts, boathouses and permits
  • Lake access lots and association rights for off lake homes

Lakes I know

I appraise lake property across the metro, including Prior Lake and Spring Lake in Scott County, the lakes of Carver County, White Bear Lake, and lakes in Dakota, Anoka and Washington counties.

Common questions

How do you value frontage?

With sales. I look for lake sales on the same or similar lakes and support any adjustment for frontage, depth or slope with what buyers have actually paid.

Do you appraise seasonal cabins outside the metro?

Ask. Assignments just beyond the metro can often be arranged.

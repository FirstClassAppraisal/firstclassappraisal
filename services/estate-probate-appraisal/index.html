Estate and Probate Appraisals

When someone passes away, the family, the personal representative or the estate attorney often needs to know what the home was worth. I provide clear, well supported estate appraisals and handle them with care.

When you may need one

  • Settling an estate through probate
  • Establishing a value as of the date of death, which can matter for taxes and the property's basis
  • Dividing property fairly between heirs
  • Selling, keeping or buying out a family home

Current or past dates

I can appraise a home as of today or as of an earlier date, such as the date of death. For a past date, I use sales from around that time and the condition of the home as it was then, as best it can be established.

Working with attorneys and families

I work directly with personal representatives, families and attorneys. If questions come up later, I am available to explain the report, and I also provide expert testimony when needed. I have been appraising Minnesota homes since 1991.

Common questions

Does the house need to be cleaned out first?

No. I can appraise the home as it is. Let me know if there are areas that are hard to reach.

Can you appraise a home as of a date several years ago?

Yes. Tell me the date you need, and I will confirm what information I will need.

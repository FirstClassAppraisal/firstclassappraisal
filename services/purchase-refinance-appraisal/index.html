Purchase and Refinance Appraisals in the Twin Cities

If you are buying a home or refinancing, your lender needs an appraisal that is accurate, well supported and delivered on time. That is what I do.

What I do

I inspect the home inside and out, measure it, photograph it and research recent sales in the neighborhood. Then I prepare a report that meets lender and secondary market requirements, including the new UAD 3.6 report format.

Who orders these

Most purchase and refinance appraisals are ordered by your lender or through an appraisal management company. If you would like me to do your appraisal, you can ask your lender whether they can use me or send me the order.

Why it matters to work with an experienced appraiser

  • 35 years appraising Minnesota homes, since 1991
  • 14 years as a national review appraiser for a large bank, so I know what underwriters look for
  • HUD FHA Roster appraiser
  • Typical turnaround of 5 to 7 business days, with rush service sometimes available

Common questions

Can I pick my own appraiser for a mortgage?

Usually the lender or its management company assigns the appraiser. You can ask your loan officer whether they can work with me.

What if the appraisal comes in low?

A low value usually means the sales support a lower price. Your lender can explain your options, which may include renegotiating or asking for a reconsideration of value with new sales data.
